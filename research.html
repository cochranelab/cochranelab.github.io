RESEARCH

Build. Deliver.
Control.

Our research develops technologies for constructing complex genomes, moving them into new biological contexts, and controlling what engineered systems do once they arrive.

01 / PHAGES

Synthetic Phage Genomics

Developing bottom-up approaches to reconstruct, modularise and systematically engineer bacteriophage genomes, with a focus on Pseudomonas phages and the design rules that govern genome compatibility.

02 / SAFE SYSTEMS

Controllable & Traceable Phages

Engineering replication control, attribution and biocontainment technologies to support responsible deployment of engineered phages.

03 / DELIVERY

Cross-Kingdom Engineering

Building broad-host DNA and capsid-based delivery platforms spanning microbes, plants and cellular compartments.

04 / ORGANELLES

Synthetic Organelles & Genomes

Constructing and delivering large organelle genomes to expand the engineering space beyond the nucleus.

Sequence is the starting material. Biology is the design space.
